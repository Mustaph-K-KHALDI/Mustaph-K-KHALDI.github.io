Curriculum Vitae

Researcher. Engineer. Educator.

I am a control systems researcher, engineer, and educator with a PhD in Systems & Control Engineering from King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM). My professional background combines research in advanced control and data-driven modeling with practical experience in industrial automation, university teaching, and technical training.

My work focuses on connecting mathematical modeling and control theory with engineering applications, from nonlinear industrial processes and intelligent systems to PLC-based automation and applied artificial intelligence. Alongside research and engineering, I am committed to engineering education and the development of practical technical skills.
