My teaching approach emphasizes connecting theoretical foundations with hands-on learning through laboratory experiments, programming, simulation, and engineering projects. I focus on developing students' conceptual understanding, analytical thinking, and practical problem-solving skills.

05 / Teaching Approach

Teaching Approach

My teaching philosophy centers on connecting theoretical foundations with practical applications. I encourage active learning through laboratory experiments, programming, simulation, and project-based activities, helping students develop analytical thinking and independent problem-solving skills.

I emphasize continuous student engagement through discussions, practical exercises, and individualized guidance. Drawing on my experience in laboratory instruction, engineering case studies, and student mentoring, I aim to create a supportive learning environment where students can confidently apply theoretical knowledge to real-world challenges.

Active Learning

Laboratory work, programming, and simulation.

Project-Based Education

Engineering case studies and applied problems.

Student Mentoring

Guidance, supervision, and independent learning.

Continuous Feedback

Discussion, interaction, and individualized support.
